    Aurelia Grand Craps

    How to Play

    The Game

    The come-out roll: 7 or 11 wins the Pass Line; 2, 3 or 12 is craps. Any other number becomes the point and the puck turns ON. Roll the point again before a 7 to win; a 7 first is a seven-out and the dice pass.

    Controls

    • Click a betting area to place the selected chip; click again to add.
    • Right-click a bet to take it down. Contract bets (Pass once a point is set, Come once traveled) stay up.
    • Odds: with a point on, click the strip behind the Pass Line, or turn on Odds mode and click Pass / Don't Pass / a number holding your Come bet.
    • Shift-click a place, buy, hardway or Big 6/8 bet to turn it ON or OFF.
    • Drag to orbit the camera; wheel to zoom.

    Bets ON / OFF & working

    • Place, buy, Big 6/8 and hardway bets are OFF on the come-out unless switched on in More or Settings. Come odds are off on the come-out.
    • Payouts round down to the dollar. Table limits $5 – $5,000; free odds up to 3-4-5×.
